Cryptocurrency price forecasting and volatility analysis

Dec 2025 – Apr 2026
PythonPandasNumPyScikit-learnTensorFlow/KerasXGBoostGARCHHTMLCSSJavaScriptChart.js
+
  • Compared 5 models (Linear Regression, Random Forest, XGBoost, KNN, LSTM) on 72K+ hourly BTC/USDT records from 2017 to 2025, with an 80/20 chronological split.
  • The LSTM predicted next-day price best: MAE 148.70, RMSE 189.84, R² 0.9962. Tree-based models scored negative R².
  • For volatility, an LSTM on Kalman-filtered sequences reached MAE 0.000657, 71% lower error than AR(1) and 97% lower than GARCH(1,1).
  • Built a dual-layer LSTM with dropout and shipped an interactive dashboard for price, volatility and model performance.

+
